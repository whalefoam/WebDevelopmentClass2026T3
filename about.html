About Webseum

What is Webseum?

Webseum is a web museum where the visitors make the art. You get one minute to draw on a blank canvas, and when the time is up your artwork is added to the gallery.

Why one minute?

Short timed drawings, often called gesture drawings, are a common exercise in art classes. The time limit makes artists focus on shapes and movement instead of small details.

How to use it

  1. Draw on the canvas on the Create page. The timer starts with your first mark.
  2. When the timer reaches 0:00 you are sent to the Gallery.
  3. Use Prev. and Next to look through the artworks.
